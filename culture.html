Our Culture

Serious work, playful hearts 💛

We believe the best international collaboration happens when academics, researchers, and operators feel free to be themselves. That's why we travel with a mascot team — and a culture built on curiosity, generosity, and rigour.

Our Mission Meet the Mascots
Mission · Vision · Values

What we stand for 🌟

Mission

To make international academic and research collaboration effortless for every master, doctor, and institution we serve — no matter where they are.

Vision

A world where a research question raised in one country can be answered with partners on three continents in a single working week.

Values

Rigour without rigidity. Generosity in our partnerships. Privacy as a default, not a feature. Joy in the work — every single day.

Corporate Social Responsibility

Giving back, the GAMD way 💛

We commit 1% of revenue, 1% of equity, and 1% of employee time to causes aligned with our mission. In practice that means:

  • Free research-grant facilitation for early-career scholars from underrepresented regions
  • Open-access publishing support for member institutions
  • Pro bono consulting for educational non-profits
  • Carbon-aware event planning for every forum we host
